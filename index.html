Koen Bresters

Graduate student at Freie Universität Berlin, as part of the Berlin Mathematical School. I'm interested in applications of (\(\infty\)-) categorical and (higher) algebraic tools to algebraic topology, algebraic geometry and mathematical logic. Currently I'm working on relating the sheaf-based homology theory considered in my bachelor thesis to the notion of shape of \(\infty\)-topoi, which is expected to reveal further properties and comparison results.
